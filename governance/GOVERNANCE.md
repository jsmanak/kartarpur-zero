# Civic Operating System: Governance, Sortition, and Mediation

## The Panch (Liquid Sortition Council)
* **Composition:** 5 to 7 citizens randomly selected via cryptographic Verifiable Random Functions (VRF) every six months.
* **Rotation:** Half the council rotates every 90 days for operational continuity.
* **Authority:** Balancing macro resources (compute allocations, civil works, seasonal cultivation); zero power to override the Charter.

## Multi-Tier Mediation: The Sant-Sipahi Protocol
1. **Tier 1 (Telemetry Audit):** Objective verification of contested physical resource logs using ambient sensor telemetry.
2. **Tier 2 (Restorative Darbar):** Face-to-face reconciliation facilitated by peer mediators, centering on restitution and social repair.
3. **Tier 3 (Node Referendum):** Node-wide sortition panel for unresolved anti-social friction. Remedies include mandatory cooling-off sabbaticals or inter-node transfer.

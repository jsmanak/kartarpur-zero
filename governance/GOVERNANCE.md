### Engineering Brief: Issue #1 Resolution

Issue #1 highlighted a critical vulnerability in our post-scarcity governance framework: the lack of a standardized, non-carceral protocol for handling individuals experiencing acute psychological breaks, neurochemical imbalances, or behavioral volatility that poses an immediate threat to themselves or others. 

Traditional punitive or carceral models rely on cages, punitive isolation, and armed enforcement—mechanisms fundamentally incompatible with Kartarpur-0’s post-scarcity axiom. 

To solve this, **Section 2.04** operationalizes a **biocybernetic and restorative containment** model. It treats acute volatility not as a moral failing or criminal act requiring punishment, but as an **environmental systems failure** and a medical/neurological emergency. Containment is achieved via non-lethal, automated environmental dampening and immediate deployment of peer-led restorative circles, prioritizing physical safety, metabolic stabilization, and communal rehabilitation without creating police infrastructure or deprivation cells.

Below is the production-ready amendment text for integration into `governance/GOVERNANCE.md`.

```markdown
## Section 2.04: Acute Life-Support Defense and Restorative Containment

### 2.04.1 Axiom of Non-Carceral Safety
The Kartarpur-0 Commune explicitly rejects carceral logic, punitive confinement, retributive justice, and permanent or armed security apparatuses. When an individual's behavior, neurochemical dysregulation, or psychological state results in an immediate, physical threat to the safety of others or themselves, intervention shall be governed strictly by protocols of **acute life-support defense, environmental dampening, and restorative containment**. At no point shall intervention incorporate punitive isolation, deprivation of basic physiological needs (nourishment, hydration, thermal comfort, or communication), or permanent civic disenfranchisement.

### 2.04.2 Automated and Environmental Dampening (Tier-1 Response)
Upon the sensor-verified or peer-reported initiation of acute physical danger or severe psychiatric crisis:
1. **Zonal Isolation Without Isolation:** Autonomous building management systems shall utilize non-harmful environmental controls—such as localized acoustic dampening, dynamic architectural redirection (smart-partition routing), and gravity-assisted kinetic barriers (e.g., non-rigid, high-tensile energy-absorption mesh)—to physically separate the individual from potential targets without sealing them in a static, unmonitored enclosure.
2. **Environmental De-escalation:** Atmospheric composition within the immediate sector may be dynamically adjusted to optimal calming parameters (e.g., precise ambient oxygen-nitrogen-negative ion balancing, circadian lighting shifts) to assist in baseline physiological down-regulation.
3. **Resource Continuity:** Life-support systems (water, caloric intake, atmospheric conditioning, and medical telemetry feeds) shall remain fully active and unimpeded within the containment zone.

### 2.04.3 Bio-Empathic and Peer Intervention Teams (Tier-2 Response)
Within 180 seconds of a Tier-1 environmental containment event, the automated dispatch router shall summon the **Restorative Containment and Care (RCC) Guild**:
1. **Composition:** The RCC team shall consist of rotating, peer-elected community members trained in crisis de-escalation, somatic psychology, neurochemistry, and non-violent physical holding. Armed personnel are structurally prohibited; RCC operators carry only bio-monitoring gear, metabolic stabilizers, and communicative interfaces.
2. **Consensus Restoration:** The primary objective of the RCC is immediate trauma-informed de-escalation, listening, and identification of the root catalyst (e.g., somatic illness, algorithmic overload, interpersonal trauma, metabolic crash).
3. **Voluntary Medical Sanctuary:** If required, the individual shall be escorted—never dragged or forced under duress—to the Commune’s Life-Support Sanctuary for holistic neurological monitoring and restorative sleep protocols.

### 2.04.4 Post-Crisis Restorative Protocol (Tier-3 Response)
Once somatic and psychological stabilization is achieved (typically within 24–72 hours):
1. **Abolition of the Trial:** No guilt-finding hearings, tribunals, or permanent records of infraction shall be created or archived. The event is classified as a *systemic health and social cohesion failure*.
2. **Circle of Integration:** A restorative circle comprising the affected individual, impacted community members, RCC facilitators, and algorithmic commune ombudsmen shall convene. The focus shall remain strictly forward-looking:
   - What environmental, metabolic, or social inputs triggered the crisis?
   - How must the commune's physical infrastructure, resource distribution, or social support webs adapt to prevent recurrence?
3. **Communal Restitution & Re-weaving:** Instead of community service as punishment, the community assumes collective responsibility for adjusting the individual's load-bearing parameters (e.g., modifying work-node assignments, upgrading living-space acoustic isolation, or adjusting neuro-pharmacological support regimens).

### 2.04.5 Oversight and Algorithmic Auditing
All Tier-1 environmental containment actions and Tier-2 RCC interventions are logged to the public-verifiable Commune Audit Ledger (anonymized to protect medical privacy). The Civic Infrastructure Working Group shall review all deployment metrics monthly to ensure that automated dampening parameters remain strictly non-injurious and that no drift toward carceral creep occurs within the system architecture.
```

# The Covenant of the Commons: Foundational Charter

## Preamble
We establish this enclave not as a retreat from civilization, but as an open-source prototype for post-scarcity human flourishing.

Anchored in three eternal pillars:
1. **Kirat (Chosen Purpose & Craft):** Freedom from forced labor is not freedom from effort. Conscious, physical, and intellectual contribution is an intrinsic good.
2. **Vand Chhako (The Sovereign Dividend):** All automated production, energy capture, compute cycles, and caloric yields are held in common trust.
3. **Seva (Status Decoupled from Wealth):** Civic honor belongs to those who nurture the commons, care for the vulnerable, teach the young, and resolve discord.

## Article I: The Inviolable Baselines
* **The Universal Physical Dividend:** Unconditional right to nutrition (via Langar), acoustic and environmental privacy, restorative medicine, and education.
* **The Sanctity of the Private Hearth:** Living dwellings are private sanctuaries. Home automation is strictly opt-in and client-side zero-knowledge.
* **The Right to Walk Away:** An open egress fund guarantees logistical and material support for any member wishing to transition out or relocate.
